2. Website Visitors & Cookies

3. Google & YouTube API Services Disclosure (Limited Use Compliance)

Our internal application (video-engine) uses YouTube API Services (including the YouTube Data API v3 and YouTube Analytics API) strictly to manage, upload, caption, and analyze video performance on YouTube channels owned and operated by Qevorin, MB.

  • Google Privacy Policy: By interacting with our YouTube content or API integration, you acknowledge that Google collects and processes data in accordance with the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Data Accessed & Stored: For authorized first-party corporate accounts, our application accesses channel metadata, video upload endpoints, public comment threads, and channel analytics metrics (views, watch time, retention). OAuth tokens are stored encrypted at rest on restricted local/server infrastructure controlled solely by Qevorin, MB.
  • Limited Use Statement: Our use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We never sell, rent, or transfer Google/YouTube user data to third parties, advertising networks, or external data brokers.
  • Revoking Access: Authorized users may revoke our application's access to their Google and YouTube data at any time via the Google Security Settings page at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. Upon revocation or request, all stored tokens and cached API metrics are permanently deleted within 24 hours.

4. Meta (Facebook & Instagram Graph API) Disclosure & Data Deletion

Our application uses the Meta Graph API and Instagram Graph API to publish video content (Reels/Videos), read public engagement metrics, and manage comments on Facebook Pages and Instagram Professional accounts owned by Qevorin, MB.

  • We do not harvest, sell, or store personal profile data of external Facebook or Instagram users.
  • User Data Deletion Instructions: If you have interacted with our Facebook Page or Instagram account (e.g., via public comments) and wish to request deletion of any data processed by our integration, send an email to info@qevorin.lt with the subject line Meta Data Deletion Request, or remove the app connection directly in your Facebook Settings under Settings & Privacy → Settings → Apps and Websites. All associated logs or cached identifiers will be purged within 48 hours.

5. TikTok Developer API (Login Kit & Content Posting API) Disclosure

Our application integrates with TikTok for Developers (Login Kit and Content Posting API) to authenticate accounts owned by Qevorin, MB (`@chronicleofhumanity`), upload video content, and retrieve first-party video performance statistics (`user.info.basic`, `user.info.stats`, `video.list`, `video.upload`, `video.publish`).

  • Authentication tokens and basic account statistics are used exclusively for automated publishing and internal performance reporting of Qevorin, MB media channels.
  • We never share TikTok API data or Personally Identifiable Information (PII) with external third parties. You may revoke TikTok authorization at any time inside the TikTok mobile app under Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Apps and services permissions.

6. Your Rights Under GDPR

As an entity established in the European Union (Lithuania), we comply with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). You have the right to request access to, rectification of, or erasure of any personal data held by us, as well as the right to lodge a complaint with the State Data Protection Inspectorate of Lithuania (Valstybinė duomenų apsaugos inspekcija). To exercise any privacy rights, contact info@qevorin.lt.
